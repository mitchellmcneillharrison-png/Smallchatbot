Tiny Sports Bot — a GPT built from scratch

A ~5M-parameter decoder-only Transformer, hand-written in PyTorch and re-implemented in plain JavaScript. Trained on thousands of sports question/answer pairs — and it runs entirely in your browser, no server.

⚠️ This is a sports bot. It knows hundreds of athletes, 100+ teams (full NBA & NFL plus MLB, NHL, and major soccer clubs), leagues, championships, and the rules of many sports — plus a little basic knowledge (simple arithmetic, greetings). It's trained on many wordings of each fact, so rephrasings usually work. Ask about non-sports topics and it will confidently make something up. It's a demo of the architecture, not a real assistant.

How does it work?

Every question is turned into word tokens, framed as <user> … <bot>, and fed through the same from-scratch Transformer (multi-head causal self-attention, LayerNorm, feed-forward, residuals — all hand-written). The model then predicts the answer one word at a time until it emits an end-of-answer token. The whole forward pass runs in JavaScript on your device; the trained weights ship as a static JSON file.

Runs 100% client-side. Full source, training code, and dataset are in the repository.
