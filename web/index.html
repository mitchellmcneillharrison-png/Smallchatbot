What am I looking at?

This is a character-level model with only a few hundred thousand parameters, trained on a single short story. At that scale it mostly learns the texture of the text — plausible letter and word patterns — rather than real meaning, so expect dream-like fragments, not coherent prose. That's the point: it's small enough to read the entire implementation and watch every matrix multiply that turns a prompt into text. Lower the temperature for safer, more repetitive output; raise it for more surprising (and more garbled) results.

Runs 100% client-side. Source, training loop, and the PyTorch model are in the repository.
